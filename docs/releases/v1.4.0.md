# 🧭 v1.4.0 — One Project, Every Deliverable

**Riser drawings, RemoteLink programming, worksheets, and door charts — together in one working project.**

## ✨ The integrations, united

- **Edit your riser in the app.** Lay out devices, reconnect wiring, adjust cable routes, add notes, and update the title block from the RISER tab.
- **Keep RemoteLink in the same workflow.** Account, user, keypad, connection, and zone-type settings stay alongside your design, with a live receipt before generation.
- **Generate the full handoff.** Export worksheets, door charts, encrypted RemoteLink accounts, and revision-matched riser PDFs at 24×36 and 11×17 plus an editable SVG.

## 🖨️ Cleaner charts and worksheets

- **Print-ready door charts.** Preserves the latest chart page grouping and correctly numbered AUX POWER / power-supply labels, including mixed 8- and 16-port expanders.
- **Complete hardware lists.** Retains numeric splitter ordering, full splitter coverage, and worksheet trimming after the last real hardware block.

## 💾 Your work stays together

- **Older projects still open.** Original, RemoteLink-only, and riser-only projects migrate into the combined format.
- **One save preserves both integrations.** RemoteLink settings, zone overrides, riser connections, manual routes, and markup survive save/reopen and crash recovery.
- **Older builds cannot silently strip new fields.** Combined projects use schema 3. Keep an original project copy if you need to work in an older app.

## 🛡️ Review before programming

RemoteLink keeps its safe defaults and verifies the encrypted account against your selections before writing it. No inherited arming schedule or automatic arm/disarm is enabled by default. Review the receipt and imported account before sending programming to a panel.

Project and summary files contain readable panel user codes; store them in approved project storage.
