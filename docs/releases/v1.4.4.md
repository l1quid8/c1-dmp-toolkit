# v1.4.4 — Saved riser recovery and MSP/RSP grouping

- Opening an outdated, complete, automatically generated scene now rebuilds its invalid location geometry instead of layering new boxes over old ones. Recovery is limited to scenes with no manually edited electrical geometry; existing markup and title data survive.
- Synchronization removes unused generated location frames and drawing routes whose electrical connections no longer exist. Auto-layout recognizes renamed frames by location, even when their stable IDs retain an older spelling.
- Manually positioned objects and live manual routes are retained. Unused manual location boxes and overlapping location boxes now produce jump-to warnings.
- In-memory recovery enables Save. The source session is not overwritten on open; normal recovery-file handling and explicit Save remain in effect.
- RSP locations explicitly entered as `MSP`, `AT MSP`, or `SAME AS MSP` share the control panel's physical-location module. RSP-1 and RSP-2 can both share that module. Explicitly different rooms and unknown locations are not reassigned by RSP number.

## Acceptance and rollback

Test the saved-scene path: open a legacy scene in the full editor, verify obsolete frames/routes are gone, save a separate copy, reopen it, and inspect its export. Also verify unchanged electrical connections, preserved manual geometry/markup, and an unchanged source file. Fresh-layout previews alone are insufficient.

Riverside's saved scene recovers to seven occupied location modules with no overlapping location boxes and thirteen live routes. Its existing occupied-port and routing-contact warnings remain visible; recovery does not alter topology to hide those warnings.

Before replacing the native app, run the full suite, verify the bundle signature/version, and confirm the app is closed. Retain v1.4.3 for rollback. Windows native interaction requires a separate release check.
